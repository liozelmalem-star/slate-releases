# Welcome to Slate

Slate is one home for every AI coding tool you use. It shows every AI coding session on your Mac live, and gives all 16 agents one memory, one set of skills and rules, and one set of connections. Your work stays on your Mac.

It is for developers who use two or more AI coding tools, often at the same time. Each tool keeps its own sessions, memory, rules and connections, and none of them shows the others. Slate puts them in one place.

## What Slate does

* **Every session, live.** [Sessions](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/sessions.md) lists every AI coding session on this Mac, from any of the 16 agents, including the ones you started in your own terminal. The sessions that need you come first.
* **One memory for every agent.** What one agent learns, the next one recalls. Slate captures it from the sessions themselves, and every connected agent reads and writes it. See [Memory](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/memory.md).
* **Skills and rules, written once.** [Toolkit](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/toolkit.md) writes your skills and instructions into each agent's own setup.
* **One set of connections.** [Connect](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/connect.md) holds your agents, their accounts, MCP servers and integrations for every tool.
* **Your own agents, no API keys.** Slate runs the command-line tools you already installed and signed in to. See [Agents](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/agents.md).

Around these sit [Home](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/home.md), [Projects](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/projects.md), [Tasks](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/tasks.md), [Automations](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/automations.md), [Artifacts](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/artifacts.md), [Widgets](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/widgets.md) and [Activity](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/activity.md).

## Your work stays on your Mac

You sign in once, with Google or GitHub. The account holds your name, email and plan. Your docs, sessions and memory stay in files on your Mac. Your work leaves it only when you share a workspace, or when an agent you run sends it to that agent's own provider. See [Privacy and security](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/privacy-and-security.md).

Slate is free for individuals. Team plans, for sharing a workspace, are coming. See [Licensing and updates](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/licensing.md).
