Settings

Settings is the last item in the left rail. Every tab is about this computer. Which AI tool a workspace runs, under which account, and what it can reach are in Connect.

TabWhat it holds
AccountWho you are signed in as, and your plan. See Licensing & Updates.
WorkspacesEvery workspace on this computer: who can see it, where its file lives, how big it is. Open one to share, rename or manage it. See Workspaces.
AppearanceLight or dark, in colour or in grey. See Themes.
TerminalFont size, line height, cursor shape and blink, scrollback, copy on select. See Sessions.
KeyboardEvery shortcut in one list. The one place to change or remove one.
AboutVersion, updates, logs, links, and what Slate may send. See Licensing & Updates and Privacy & Security.

What Slate runs on its own is no longer a Settings tab. Since 2026-11-06 it is the Built-in section of the Automations place. See Automations.

Slate is in alpha. Your data stays on your machine.
