Settings

View as markdownOpen the .md copy of this page

Settings holds what belongs to you and this computer: your account, the workspaces on this Mac, the theme, your shortcuts and the app itself. Open it from the round button near the foot of the rail. What one workspace runs, under which account, is in Connect. What Slate runs on its own is in Automations, under Built-in.

TabWhat it holds
AccountWho you are, and your plan
WorkspacesEvery workspace on this computer: who can see it, where it lives and how big it is
AppearanceLight or dark
KeyboardEvery shortcut, and the one place to change it
AboutVersion, updates, logs, and what Slate may send

Account#

  • Your picture, name and email, from Google or GitHub.
  • Display name: what people in your workspaces see on everything you publish. Save appears when you change it.
  • Plan: the Free and Team cards. Team is coming; Tell me when it's ready records that you want it. See Licensing and updates.
  • Sign out.
  • Delete my account forever…: removes your Slate account and everything you shared to a workspace. Everything on this Mac stays. You type your email to confirm. See Privacy and security.

Workspaces#

Lists every workspace on this computer. Click one to open its dialog, with the tabs General, Members (a shared workspace you manage) and Storage. New workspace makes one. Sync now pushes your work to shared workspaces and pulls your teammates' at once; it also happens every two minutes. See Workspaces and Your data and export.

Appearance#

Two themes, one dark and one light. Click a card to use it; the one in use says Active. See Themes.

Keyboard#

Every command that has a shortcut. Click a key to record a new one. See Keyboard shortcuts.

About#

  • Your version, and whether an update is waiting, with Check now and Install & restart. See Licensing and updates.
  • Website, Documentation and Source open in your browser.
  • Logs opens the folder of app and agent logs. Attach the latest file when you report a problem.
  • Open-source licences lists the open-source software Slate includes.
  • Report a problem opens the report dialog. See Privacy and security.
  • Help improve Slate holds two switches, Anonymous usage counts and Crash reports. Both are off until you turn them on. New id replaces the random id they are sent under.

You, on the rail#

The last button on the rail is you: your picture, or your initials when there is none. Its menu shows your name, your email, which provider you signed in with, Account (opens Settings → Account) and Sign out.

Slate downloads your picture once and keeps a copy on this Mac. Signing out removes the copy.
