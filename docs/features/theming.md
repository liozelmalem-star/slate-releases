# Themes

Slate has two themes: **Mint**, which is dark, and **Forest**, which is light. Choose one in **Settings → Appearance**. Mint is the default.

## Switch between light and dark

Press **⌘⇧L**, or choose **Toggle light / dark theme** in the command palette. It switches between Mint and Forest, and the **Active** card in Settings → Appearance follows.

## What the colours mean

Most of Slate is neutral grey. The brand green marks only a few things: Slate's mark, the main button, what is selected, and the rail place you are on.

* **State keeps its own colours.** Amber means something needs you. Red means it failed, or the next step cannot be undone. Green means done or connected. A state always has a word or an icon too.
* **The running light is lime.** A session or a run that is working shows a lime dot.
* **Terminal output stays in colour.** A command's red still means a failure.

## Workspace colours

A workspace wears one of nine colours: **Cobalt**, **Lagoon**, **Iris**, **Silver**, **Orchid**, **Fjord**, **Heather**, **Wisteria** and **Graphite**. Choose one under **Colour** in the workspace's dialog in **Settings → Workspaces**. The colour marks the workspace in the switcher and in Settings, always beside its name. It is yours alone: other members keep their own.

## Make Slate bigger or smaller

**⌘+** and **⌘−** zoom the whole app in steps of 10%, from 80% to 140%. It starts at 110%. The size is saved on this computer, for every workspace. In a terminal the same keys change only that terminal's font.

There is no custom theme and no colour picker for parts of the app.
