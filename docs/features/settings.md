# Settings

**Settings** is the last item in the left rail. Every tab is about this computer. Which agent a workspace runs, under which account, and what it can reach are in [Connect](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/connect.md).

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Who you are signed in as, your plan, sign-out, and **Delete my account forever**, which removes your Slate account from the cloud and keeps everything on this Mac. See [Licensing & Updates](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/licensing.md). |
| **Workspaces** | Every workspace on this computer: who can see it, where its file lives, how big it is. Open one to share, rename or manage it. See [Workspaces](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/workspaces.md). |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark. See [Themes](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/theming.md). |
| **Terminal** | Font size, line height, cursor shape and blink, scrollback, copy on select. See [Sessions](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/sessions.md). |
| **Keyboard** | Every shortcut in one list. The one place to change or remove one. See [Keyboard shortcuts](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/shortcuts.md). |
| **About** | Version, updates, logs, links, and what Slate may send. See [Licensing & Updates](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/licensing.md) and [Privacy & Security](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/privacy-and-security.md). |

What Slate runs on its own is the **Built-in** section of the [Automations](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/loop.md) place, not a Settings tab. See [Automations](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/automations.md).
