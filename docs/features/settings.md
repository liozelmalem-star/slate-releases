# Settings

**Settings** holds what belongs to you and this computer: your account, the workspaces on this Mac, the theme, your shortcuts and the app itself. Open it from the round button near the foot of the rail. What one workspace runs, under which account, is in [Connect](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/connect.md). What Slate runs on its own is in [Automations](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/automations.md), under **Built-in**.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Who you are, and your plan |
| **Workspaces** | Every workspace on this computer: who can see it, where it lives and how big it is |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark |
| **Keyboard** | Every shortcut, and the one place to change it |
| **About** | Version, updates, logs, and what Slate may send |

## Account

* Your picture, name and email, from Google or GitHub.
* **Display name**: what people in your workspaces see on everything you publish. **Save** appears when you change it.
* **Plan**: the **Free** and **Team** cards. Team is coming; **Tell me when it's ready** records that you want it. See [Licensing and updates](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/licensing.md).
* **Sign out**.
* **Delete my account forever…**: removes your Slate account and everything you shared to a workspace. Everything on this Mac stays. You type your email to confirm. See [Privacy and security](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/privacy-and-security.md#your-account).

## Workspaces

Lists every workspace on this computer. Click one to open its dialog, with the tabs **General**, **Members** (a shared workspace you manage) and **Storage**. **New workspace** makes one. **Sync now** pushes your work to shared workspaces and pulls your teammates' at once; it also happens every two minutes. See [Workspaces](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/workspaces.md) and [Your data and export](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/data-and-export.md).

## Appearance

Two themes, one dark and one light. Click a card to use it; the one in use says **Active**. See [Themes](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/theming.md).

## Keyboard

Every command that has a shortcut. Click a key to record a new one. See [Keyboard shortcuts](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/shortcuts.md#change-a-shortcut).

## About

* Your version, and whether an update is waiting, with **Check now** and **Install & restart**. See [Licensing and updates](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/licensing.md).
* **Website**, **Documentation** and **Source** open in your browser.
* **Logs** opens the folder of app and agent logs. Attach the latest file when you report a problem.
* **Open-source licences** lists the open-source software Slate includes.
* **Report a problem** opens the report dialog. See [Privacy and security](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/privacy-and-security.md#reporting-a-problem).
* **Help improve Slate** holds two switches, **Anonymous usage counts** and **Crash reports**. Both are off until you turn them on. **New id** replaces the random id they are sent under.

## You, on the rail

The last button on the rail is you: your picture, or your initials when there is none. Its menu shows your name, your email, which provider you signed in with, **Account** (opens Settings → Account) and **Sign out**.

Slate downloads your picture once and keeps a copy on this Mac. Signing out removes the copy.
