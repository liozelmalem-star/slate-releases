Features

Trash

Trash holds the docs and apps you moved there. They stay until you restore them or delete them forever. Open it from Trash in the left rail, above Settings.

What lands here

  • A doc, with every doc inside it. Use ⋯ → Move to Trash in the Projects tree, or Move to Trash in the doc.
  • An app. ⋯ → Move to Trash keeps every version. An app you have not opened or changed for 60 days moves here by itself, after a 7-day warning in its list (Apps).

Each row says when it was moved and where it came from: "Moved to Trash 5 minutes ago · from Acme". A doc that held others shows "N inside"; expand it to see them. Click a title to read it first.

Restore, or delete forever

  • Restore puts the doc back where it was, with everything inside it. If its parent is now a Past branch, it is filed there, and Trash says so ("Restored into Past branch …").
  • Delete forever… asks first and cannot be undone. A doc's nested docs go with it; Past branches inside it are kept. An app loses every version and the data it kept.

Tick rows to act on several: Restore (N) and Delete forever (N). Select all ticks every row shown. With nothing ticked, Empty Trash deletes everything forever, after the same question.

Filter by title narrows the list. With a filter on, the button reads Delete forever · N shown and removes only the rows you can see forever.

In a shared workspace

Trash belongs to the open workspace. Moving a doc you wrote to Trash removes it from every member's copy on their next sync. A teammate's doc under it stays; when you delete yours forever, theirs moves up to your doc's parent (Working in a Shared Workspace).

Slate is in alpha. Your data stays on your machine.
