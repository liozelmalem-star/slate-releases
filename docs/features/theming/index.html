Themes

Slate ships four themes, two dark and two light, chosen in Settings → Appearance. Picking one sets both the light/dark mode and the whole surface palette, and the change applies across the app immediately.

The four themes

ThemeModeLook
StrataDarkThe default. A near-black ground carrying the wordmark's hue at low chroma
ChalkLightStrata's counterpart — the same hue, on the light curve
OnyxDarkMonochrome. A true black background, everything above it in grey
GraphiteLightMonochrome. Off-white paper, near-black ink

Strata and Chalk are the coloured pair: alongside the surface they carry the five colours of Slate's mark, which are what tell doc types, fact kinds, memory-graph nodes and page covers apart.

Onyx and Graphite take the colour out. They are not a different design — they are the same two themes with every hue removed, including the five of the mark. The categorical distinction survives as five steps of grey instead of five colours, and Slate's own logo follows the theme rather than staying the one coloured object on the screen.

Two things are worth knowing before you pick one:

  • Five greys are harder to tell apart at a glance than five colours. Every one of these marks sits beside its own label, so the label is what identifies it and the grey is a second cue — but a monochrome memory graph is a flatter picture than a coloured one.
  • The green running light stays green. The small dot that says a session or a routine is working right now keeps its colour under Onyx and Graphite. It is the one exception, and it is deliberate: that dot has nothing written beside it, so in grey it was still drawn and could no longer be seen.
  • Terminal output stays in colour. A CLI's red means the build failed, and no shade of grey means that. The terminal keeps its sixteen ANSI colours under every theme.

Accent

The accent is which of the mark's five colours leads buttons, links and focus rings. It is a separate choice from the theme, so switching between light and dark keeps it.

The monochrome themes have no hue to lead with, so the accent picker is hidden while one is selected. Your choice is remembered, not discarded — pick Strata or Chalk again and it comes back.

What a theme affects

ElementBehavior
SurfacesBackground, text, panels and borders follow the selected theme
Categorical colourDoc types, fact kinds, graph nodes and page covers follow the theme's five leaves — greys under Onyx and Graphite
Accented componentsButtons, links and focus rings take the accent, or the theme's greys when it is monochrome
The Slate markColoured under Strata and Chalk, grey under Onyx and Graphite
The running lightGreen under every theme, monochrome included
TerminalSurface colours follow the theme; the ANSI colours never do

Appearance is a choice among designed themes, not a palette builder. There is no per-element colour picker and no custom theme file.
