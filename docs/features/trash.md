# Trash

**Trash** holds the docs and widgets you moved there, until you restore them or delete them forever. Open it from **Trash** on the rail, at the foot of the open workspace's icons. Each workspace has its own Trash.

## What goes to Trash

* **A doc**, with every doc inside it. Use **Move to Trash** in the doc's **⋯** menu in the Projects tree, or in the doc itself.
* **A widget**, with every version. Use **Move to Trash** in its **⋯** menu. See [Widgets](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/widgets.md).

Each row says when it moved and where it came from, such as *Moved to Trash 5 minutes ago · from Acme*. A doc that held others shows **N inside**; expand it to see them. Click a title to read it.

## Restore or delete forever

Point at a row, or move focus to it, to see **Restore** and **Delete forever…**.

* **Restore** puts the doc back where it was, with every doc that went to Trash with it. A doc inside it that you moved to Trash on its own, earlier, stays in Trash.
* **Delete forever…** asks first and cannot be undone. Docs nested inside go too. A widget loses every version and the data it kept. Memories filed under a doc deleted forever are kept, as global memories.

To act on several rows, tick them and press **Restore (N)** or **Delete forever (N)**. **Select all** ticks every row shown.

**Empty Trash**, beside the title, deletes everything in Trash forever after one question. **Filter by title** narrows the list; with a filter on, the button reads **Delete forever · N shown** and removes only the rows you see.

## In a shared workspace

Moving a doc you wrote to Trash removes it from every member's copy on their next sync. A teammate's doc inside yours stays. When you delete yours forever, theirs moves up to your doc's parent. See [Working in a shared workspace](https://liozelmalem-star.github.io/slate-releases/docs/features/workspaces/sharing.md).
