Why is the cart total off by one cent?

Found it. cartTotal in src/cart/total.ts:42 rounds every line item to cents and then adds them up. Slate has your decision from Oct 8: round once, on the total. With three items at $3.335 that is one cent too much.

The fix is to sum in cents first and round once at the end. I'd like to make that edit:

Edit /Users/dana/code/acme/acme-web/src/cart/total.tsEdit
  const cents = items.reduce((sum, i) => sum + i.price * i.qty * 100, 0);
  return Math.round(cents) / 100;
