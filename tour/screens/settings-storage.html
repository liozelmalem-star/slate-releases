Workspaces

Who can see what, where each one lives, and how big it is.

A workspace is an audience. Everything in a shared one is readable by its members; everything in an unshared one stays on this machine. Open a workspace to share it, rename it, or see where it lives.

Sign in on the Account tab to create a shared workspace. Personal works without one.
